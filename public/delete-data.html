Delete Your Data

How to remove your data or close your account in the EnVest app.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete your data and account directly from the app:

  1. Open the EnVest app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile picture to open Settings.
  3. Go to the Account & Privacy section.
  4. Select Delete My Account.
  5. Tell us the reason (optional) and review the confirmation details.
  6. Tap Confirm to submit your request.
  7. Our team reviews and processes your request, and your account is closed.
Deleting your account is permanent. Once it is processed, you will no longer be able to sign in, and you will need to create a brand-new account to use EnVest again.

What gets deleted

When your request is approved, we remove your active account, including:

Removed

  • Your account and sign-in access
  • Your profile and personal details
  • Your saved preferences and app settings
  • Your wallet and portfolio dashboard access

Kept for compliance

  • Transaction and payment records
  • Investment and portfolio records
  • Identity (KYC) records and documents

As a financial service, EnVest is required by law to keep certain records for a period of time for legal, regulatory, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. These records are retained only as the law requires and are not used to market to you.

How long it takes

Deletion requests are usually processed within a few business days. Once your account is closed, it can no longer be used to sign in or access EnVest services.

A note on re-registration

For your security, the email address and phone number linked to a deleted account are reserved and cannot be reused to open a new account.

Submit a deletion request

Enter the email address on your EnVest account and we'll start the deletion process for you.

Need help?

If you have any questions about deleting your data, or you would like us to action a request on your behalf, contact our support team at support@envest.live.
